## Documented source evidence

The official description explicitly identifies
Spatialized Canadian National Forest Inventory
(SCANFI), version 2.0.

The product is described as incorporating burned-area
and other information from preceding years through
an annual update process.

Exact source editions, observation dates, and processing
rules still require investigation.

## Available raster products

The catalogue advertises:

- 30 m GeoTIFF
- 100 m GeoTIFF
- 250 m GeoTIFF

The 30 m product is identified as the default.

The 100 m product is described as suitable
for fire-growth modelling.

The native CRS, raster grid alignment, file sizes,
and derivation of coarser resolutions require
verification from the downloaded files.

## Classification qualifications

The official catalogue states:

- Slash fuel types S-1, S-2, and S-3 are absent.
- Conifer Plantation C-6 is absent.
- Raster value 13 represents vegetated fuels without
  an effective match to an FBP fuel type.
- Raster value 11 corresponds to D-1 Leafless Aspen
  but is treated as deciduous fuel in this product.

Value 13 must not automatically be interpreted as
nonfuel, missing evidence, or a standard FBP fuel type.

The complete raster code dictionary and mapping rules
remain to be verified.

## Relationship to earlier products

2019:
- Documented at 250 m.
- Uses earlier national inventory evidence.
- Includes documented 2018 classification methodology.

2024:
- Documented at 30 m.
- Uses SCANFI, ecozones, and NBAC.
- Exact SCANFI version remains unresolved.

2026:
- Explicitly uses SCANFI 2.0.
- Available at 30 m, 100 m, and 250 m.
- Describes annual burned-area incorporation.

Common FBP terminology does not establish
identical code mappings or assignment rules.

## Westwood Hills considerations

The 2023 fire precedes this product.

Post-fire landscape conditions may influence
the classification through newer source evidence.

Whether the Westwood Hills footprint was included,
and how affected cells were classified, must be
verified directly.

## Reproducibility

This is a current-year product with an annual
update schedule.

When acquired, record:

- Available version information
- Any subsequent changes to the distribution

A later current-year release must not silently
replace the 2026 experimental input.

## Research implications

The 2026 product provides a third landscape
representation, not necessarily a third observation
in a consistent chronological series.

Evidence age, fuel classification, spatial support,
and historical landscape change must be evaluated
as separate sources of assessment instability.

Stability across these products does not constitute
a calibrated probability of correctness.
