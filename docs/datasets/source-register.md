validated dataset.

## Required verification

- Product version and release date
- Source observation dates

- Classification codes and definitions

- Relationship to the 2023 wildfire
- Potential shared source evidence
- Download format, size, and licensing

No datasets acquired as part of this inventory.

## Interpretation policy

Dataset disagreement is not automatically classification
error. Temporal landscape change, different observation
periods, classification rules, and spatial support must
be investigated separately.
